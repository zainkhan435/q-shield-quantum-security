How Q-SHIELD Detects Protocol Threats

The platform replaces computational hardness assumptions with information-theoretic quantum physics and rigorous statistical mechanics.

01

Quantum-State Simulation

Synthesizes single-qubit signature states |ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + e^(iφ)sin(θ/2)|1⟩ on the interactive 3D Bloch sphere, tracking exact pure and mixed density matrices.

02

Bell-State Entanglement

Distributes maximally entangled Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) pairs |Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2 across communication channels, forming an unbreakable correlated quantum bridge.

03

Quantum Teleportation

Executes Bennett's 3-qubit teleportation protocol to transmit signature states without physical qubit traversal, pairing joint Bell measurement with classical bit feed (c₀, c₁).

04

Projective Measurements

Verifies signature parity in mutually unbiased Pauli bases (Z, X, Y). Quantum wavefunction collapse guarantees that unauthorized inspection introduces detectable errors.

05

Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER)

Empirically measures the proportion of orthogonal measurement outcomes against the theoretical 11.0% cutoff, precisely separating natural noise from active eavesdropping.

06

Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Applies binomial standard deviation Z-scores to measurement distributions (Z ≥ 3.0σ), ensuring deterministic threat alerts with 99.73% statistical confidence without black-box ML.

CYBERSECURITY ATTACK SURFACE

Detectable Cyber Threat Vectors

Five critical attack scenarios simulated, analyzed, and audited against deterministic verification criteria.

THREAT VECTOR 01 Parity Collapse

Signature Forgery

Adversaries attempting to forge Alice's signature alter the quantum payload state (|ψ'⟩ ≠ |ψ⟩). Bob's projective verification collapses to orthogonal parity, yielding ~100% QBER and immediate signature rejection.

THREAT VECTOR 02 Entanglement Mismatch

Signer Impersonation

Untrusted adversaries attempting authentication without valid pre-shared entanglement material produce uncorrelated random distributions. Multiparty verification between Bob and Charlie flags the identity.

THREAT VECTOR 03 Nonce Collision

Replay Attacks

Adversaries capturing and re-transmitting legitimate classical bits (c₀, c₁) are detected by Q-SHIELD's cryptographic single-use UUIDv4 nonce ledger in SQLite, halting replay attempts with zero traceback.

THREAT VECTOR 04 Wavefunction Collapse

Intercept & Resend (Eve)

Adversary Eve taps the quantum channel to measure in-flight qubits. Measurement collapses the entangled Bell pair, inducing ~25% to 50% QBER, drastically exceeding the 11.0% cutoff and triggering an alert.

THREAT VECTOR 05 Depolarizing Noise

Channel Manipulation

Environmental decoherence, thermal turbulence, or physical fiber tampering forcing depolarizing errors beyond safe margins (≥15%) are classified as anomalies through statistical Z-score elevation (Z ≥ 3.0σ).
